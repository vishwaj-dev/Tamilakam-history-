# 🏛️ TAMILAKAM — History Academy

**Explore the history, culture, literature and kingdoms of ancient Tamilakam through an interactive learning experience.**

Tamilakam is a mobile-friendly history website designed to take readers on a journey through the different periods of Tamil history — from prehistoric settlements and Sangam literature to powerful dynasties, medieval kingdoms and modern Tamil identity.

## 🌟 Features

- **42 History Chapters** — Explore topics organised into six historical eras.
- **Six Historical Eras** — Follow the journey from early communities to modern Tamil history.
- **Interactive Chapter Library** — Browse and open individual history chapters.
- **Search Functionality** — Find chapters, historical figures and places.
- **Era Filters** — Navigate topics by historical period.
- **Reading Progress** — Track completed chapters with a progress indicator.
- **Visual Learning** — Explore historical illustrations and related imagery.
- **Responsive Design** — Designed for desktop and mobile screens.
- **Dark Theme** — A cinematic interface inspired by Tamil heritage.

## 📚 Historical Sections

| Section | Topics |
|---|---|
| Part I — The Beginnings | Early communities, language, archaeology and Megalithic culture |
| Part II — The Three Sangams | Sangam literature, poets, landscapes and society |
| Part III — The Ancient Kingdoms | Cheras, Cholas, Pandyas and Velir chiefs |
| Part IV — The Transition | Kalabhras, Pallavas and the Bhakti movement |
| Part V — The Imperial Age | Imperial Cholas, Pandyas, temples and administration |
| Part VI — Medieval and Modern History | Nayaks, Marathas, colonial rule and modern Tamil identity |

## 🎯 Project Purpose

This project aims to make Tamil history more accessible and engaging through a modern, visual and interactive website. It brings together historical periods, literature, personalities, archaeological themes and cultural heritage in one learning space.

## 📝 Historical Note

This is an educational learning project. Historical illustrations are used for visual representation and should not automatically be treated as authentic photographs or archaeological evidence. Some traditional accounts and historical interpretations require further research and verification.
